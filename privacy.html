Last updated: 13 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Chess Trainer ("we", "us", the "app") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, our authentication provider (Google Firebase Authentication) gives us a user ID and, depending on how you sign in, your email address and display name.
  • Your activity in the app. We store the progress you create: lessons studied, puzzle results and ratings, saved games and reviews, friends you add, and tournaments you join. This is kept so your progress follows you across devices.
  • Payment information. If you subscribe to Pro, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number; Stripe handles that. We receive your subscription status (active, cancelled, and similar) so we can unlock Pro features.
  • Linked chess accounts (optional). If you choose to import games for review, you provide a Chess.com or Lichess username. We use it only to fetch your public games from those services at your request.
  • Technical data. Like most apps, basic technical information (such as device and browser type) may be processed by our hosting and infrastructure providers to deliver and secure the app.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app: sign-in, saving your progress, syncing across devices, and unlocking features you pay for.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the app.
  • To respond to you if you contact us for support.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.

Service providers we share data with

We rely on a small number of trusted providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database/storage, and serverless functions).
  • Stripe (subscription payments and billing).
  • Our website host for serving the app.
  • YouTube when you choose to play an embedded lesson video; YouTube may set its own cookies. We also link out to Chess.com and Lichess only when you ask to import games.

The chess engine (Stockfish) runs entirely on your own device; the moves you analyse are not sent to us for that purpose.

Storage and security

Your data is stored with our infrastructure providers and protected by access rules. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • Deleting your account and data. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at [your contact email]. We will delete your account data, except anything we are required to keep (for example, billing records Stripe retains for tax and legal reasons).
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (such as access, portability, or erasure under the GDPR, or rights under the CCPA). Contact us to exercise them.
