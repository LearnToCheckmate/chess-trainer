Delete Your Account

Last updated: 13 June 2026

You can delete your Chess Trainer account and the personal data associated with it at any time. This page explains what happens and how to request it.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the data tied to it, including:

  • Your sign-in account (the user record and associated email or display name).
  • Your in-app progress: lessons studied, puzzle results and ratings, saved games and reviews, friends, and tournaments.
  • Any chess usernames you linked for game import.

What may be kept

A small amount of data may be retained where the law requires it, such as billing and transaction records held by our payment processor (Stripe) for tax and accounting purposes. These are not used to identify you in the app after deletion.

How to request deletion

  1. If you have an active Pro subscription, cancel it first (see Refunds & Cancellation) so you are not billed again.
  2. Email us from the address on your account so we can verify the request:
Email: [your contact email]

Please include the email or display name on the account. We will confirm and complete the deletion, and we aim to do so within [30] days.

Questions
