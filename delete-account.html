Delete Your Account

Last updated: [PLACEHOLDER: publication date]

You can delete your Chess Trainer account and the data tied to it at any time, from inside the app.

How to delete it

  1. If you have an active Pro subscription, open Account › Manage subscription and cancel it first, so you are not billed again.
  2. Open Account (the button at the top right of Home) and tap Delete account, then Delete my account.
  3. Google asks you to confirm it is you. If you close that window, nothing is deleted.

What gets deleted

  • Your synced progress and settings (lessons, puzzles, ratings, streaks, saved mistakes and brilliancies, Coach settings).
  • Your Quick match and Play nearby entries.
  • Your friend requests, sent and received, and your friendships.
  • The tournaments you host, and your entries in other people's tournaments.
  • Game invites nobody has accepted yet, and the video call records of all your games.
  • In games already played, your name and photo are replaced with "Deleted player" and your chat lines are removed; the moves and the result stay, because they are also your opponent's game.
  • Your sign-in account itself, last.
  • On the device you delete from: your progress, linked usernames and imported games.

What may be kept

Stripe, our payment processor, keeps billing and transaction records it is required to keep for tax and accounting. The app's own copy of your subscription status (kept by Stripe's Firebase extension) is removed when the sign-in account is deleted [PLACEHOLDER: true only once the extension's "delete Stripe customers when a user is deleted" setting is on - confirm it, or delete this sentence]. If you used the app on other devices, their local copies stay until you sign out there (choose "Remove my data from this device") or clear the site's data.
