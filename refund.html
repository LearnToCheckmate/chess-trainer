Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: 13 June 2026

This page explains how Chess Trainer Pro subscriptions renew, how to cancel, and our approach to refunds.

Contact for billing help: [your contact email]. Confirm the refund window below matches the policy you want before publishing.

Subscriptions and renewal

Chess Trainer Pro is a recurring subscription billed through Stripe. It renews automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly or yearly, as chosen at purchase) until you cancel.

How to cancel

  • You can cancel at any time from the subscription or account section inside the app, or through the Stripe billing portal link provided there.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Pro access until the end of the period you have already paid for; it is not cut off immediately.

Refunds

  • Because you can cancel anytime and keep access through the paid period, we generally do not provide refunds for partial periods.
  • As a courtesy, if you are charged for a renewal you did not intend and contact us within [14] days of that charge, we will review a refund request in good faith.
  • Nothing here limits refund or cancellation rights you have under the consumer laws that apply to you (for example, statutory withdrawal rights in the EU/UK). Those rights still apply.

Failed or disputed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Pro access may pause until payment succeeds. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us before opening a dispute so we can sort it out quickly.

How to request a refund

Email [your contact email] from the address on your account, with the approximate date of the charge. We will respond as soon as we reasonably can.
